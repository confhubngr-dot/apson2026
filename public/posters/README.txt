Poster images go in this folder, e.g. posters/p01.jpg, then put that file name in the
Sheet's "Posters" tab (image column). JPG about 1200 px on the long side, under 400 KB.
Faster alternative, no redeploy needed: upload the poster to Google Drive, share it
"Anyone with the link", and paste the Drive link into the image column instead.
Delegates tap a poster to view it full screen.
