Sponsor logos and flyers go in this folder, e.g. sponsors/acme-logo.png and sponsors/acme-flyer.jpg,
then put those file names in the Sheet's "Sponsors" tab (logo and flyer columns).
Logos: square-ish PNG, about 300 px. Flyers: JPG about 1000 px wide, under 300 KB.
No time to redeploy? Upload the image to Google Drive, share it "Anyone with the link",
and paste the Drive link into the Sheet instead — the app displays it directly.
Remember to bump the cache in sw.js (ap26-v6 -> ap26-v7) whenever you deploy new files.
